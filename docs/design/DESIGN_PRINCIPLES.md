# The Living Archive: Design Principles

**Version:** V3 Design System  
**Date:** 30 October 2026  
**Product Premise:** A contemporary digital archive for exploring Vedic material with textual seriousness, warmth, precision, and discovery.  

---

## The Eight Design Principles

### 1. Content Before Spectacle
The sacred text, its linguistic structure, and its historical-philosophical context form the unequivocal visual and experiential center. Decorative visual elements must never distract from, obscure, or compete with reading, contemplation, or search.

### 2. Expressive Restraint
Character arises from a few deeply considered details—precise typography, intentional whitespace, fine rules, and natural material contrast—rather than layers of competing visual effects. A screen should feel complete and confident without needing ambient particle storms or synthetic neon glows.

### 3. Editorial Hierarchy
In any archival passage, six distinct informational layers must be unmistakable at a glance:
1. Source & canonical reference (e.g., Atharvaveda Saunaka 12.1.1).
2. Original Devanagari text with Vedic accents.
3. Transliteration (IAST) with accurate diacritics.
4. Translation with accredited translator attribution.
5. Archival commentary, grammatical notes, or vocabulary roots.
6. AI-assisted interpretation, visibly distinguished and never masquerading as canonical source.

### 4. Materiality Without Imitation
The interface evokes the sensory dignity of ink, archival birch bark (Bhurjapatra), stone inscriptions, and walnut storage cabinets through composition, proportion, hairline rules, and subtle tactile texture. It avoids faux-aged parchment textures, fake burn edges, or pretending to be a historical physical artifact.

### 5. Motion With Purpose
Every animation must have a demonstrable rationale: explaining structural depth, confirming user action, or easing transitions between collections and passages. If an animation exists solely for decoration and slows down access to content, it must be removed.

### 6. Truthful Representation
All numbers, verse counts, audio waveforms, pitch evaluations, and relationship graphs must reflect real, verified data. The interface never displays fake live statistics, random pitch accuracy meters, or marketing counters presented as browseable records.

### 7. Responsive by Design
The mobile experience must feel authored for the hand, not merely like a desktop viewport compressed into 375 pixels. Critical reading typography, marginal annotations, and navigation must feel native and comfortable on small handheld devices.

### 8. Accessible by Default
WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance is a non-negotiable baseline. Contrast ratios must meet or exceed 4.5:1 for body text and 3.0:1 for large text and interactive components. Reduced motion preferences must be respected across all layout transitions, and no critical information may ever depend on hover alone.
